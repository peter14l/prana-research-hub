Scientific Feasibility Assessment Verdict

INSUFFICIENT FOR SPACEBORNE EARLY WARNING OR AUTONOMOUS SUPPRESSION

Spaceborne satellite thermal observations (NASA FIRMS VIIRS/MODIS, Landsat 8/9) cannot support autonomous fire tracking or suppression due to: (1) Subsurface Blindness (smoldering occurs 2-6m deep with no flaming surface heat); (2) Pixel Mismatch (375m pixel vs 10-50m² fire fissure); (3) Overpass Latency (2 passes/day, 1-3 hr lag); (4) Monsoon Blindness (85-98% cloud cover June-Sept). Defensible engineering requires ground-based continuous LWIR radiometric PTZ cameras + subsurface thermocouple probes.

Monthly Thermal Activity & Seasonal Peak

Peak anomaly frequency occurs in pre-monsoon dry season (March-May) due to desiccated refuse and intense solar heating, followed by post-monsoon winter flare-ups. Monsoon shows zero detections due to heavy rainfall and cloud cover.

Spatial Anomaly Density: Landfill vs Buffers

While Core AOI displays high density during acute blazes (10.27 det/km²), peripheral buffers register intermittent low-confidence false positives driven by industrial galvanized steel roofs and asphalt roads.
